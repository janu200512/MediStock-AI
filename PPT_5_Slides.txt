SLIDE 1 — MediStock AI
Smart Health & Supply Chain Resilience
AI-assisted medicine demand forecasting and inventory risk detection
Team: Janu Ahirwar
Track 3

SLIDE 2 — Problem
• Medicine stock-outs can disrupt healthcare delivery.
• Overstocking can increase expiry and inventory waste.
• Manual planning may react after a shortage becomes visible.
• Healthcare teams need an early-warning view of demand, stock cover and replenishment.

SLIDE 3 — Our Solution
• Central dashboard for medicine inventory.
• AI-assisted short-term demand forecasting.
• Stock-out risk classification: Low / Medium / High / Critical.
• Suggested reorder quantity using predicted demand and supplier lead time.
• Downloadable reorder plan for procurement teams.

SLIDE 4 — How It Works & Tech
Input: historical consumption + receipts + current stock
→ feature engineering: lag demand + rolling average + day-of-week
→ Random Forest demand forecast
→ stock-cover & risk calculation
→ reorder recommendation
Tech: Python, Streamlit, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn
Prototype data is synthetic for demonstration.

SLIDE 5 — Impact & Future Scope
Expected value:
• Earlier visibility of potential shortages
• More data-driven procurement planning
• Lower risk of avoidable stock-outs
• Better control of excess inventory/expiry
